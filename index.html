The Process

Swap in Three Simple Steps

No fees. No courier. Just two readers finding each other in the same city.

1

List Your Books

Upload books sitting on your shelf to your personal Nest. Add genre tags and condition details.

2

Find a Match

Browse by genre, author, or title. Add to your Wishlist and request a swap when you find the one.

3

Meet & Exchange

Connect directly with the owner to arrange a meetup. Exchange books and start reading!
